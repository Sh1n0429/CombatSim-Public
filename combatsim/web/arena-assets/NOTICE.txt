CombatSim ARENA original score and effects
=========================================
arena-score.ogg is an original E-minor aviation arena-rock cue (124 BPM,
32 bars, approximately 62 seconds). The arrangement uses double-tracked
power-chord guitars, sustained lead guitar, picked bass, analog-style pads,
and power drums with a short gated snare room. Composition and arrangement
were created for CombatSim, with an original melody.

The music was rendered offline with FluidSynth 2.4.8 and GeneralUser GS 2.0.3
by S. Christian Collins. GeneralUser GS permits private and commercial music
recordings. Source and music-production terms:
https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser
https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS/blob/main/documentation/LICENSE.txt
Only the finished music recording is bundled; the SoundFont and rendering tools
are not included or needed at runtime.

arena-engine.ogg, arena-launch.ogg and arena-impact.ogg were synthesized for
this project. Created for use and redistribution with CombatSim.
